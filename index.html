Grow your 30-day goal tree

Your mind acts on the evidence you feed it. This tree lets you watch that happen: the roots are your subconscious, the trunk is your goal, and the fruits are your stresses, solved.

Set it up once

  1. Write your 3 to 5 biggest stresses. The things on your mind at night and when you wake up.
  2. Turn each one into a fruit. Write it as if it is already solved, like "Our debt is paid off."
  3. Pick a power goal you believe. A stretch, but one your mind will not reject.

Then every day

  • Press Start today's visualization, close your eyes and feel your fruits as real for 2 minutes.
  • Your first session each day grows your tree one day and turns a red root green. Do it again as many times as you like, and the site counts every session.
  • Miss a day and your tree starts to dim, until you come back.

Why this site exists

What is Reprogram Your Mind?

You probably just watched Rav Malik's training, How to Reprogram Your Mind for Fast Growth, and now you're wondering how to actually do it every day. That's what this site is for. It turns his tree and his 2-minute visualization into a simple daily habit you can see.

Haven't watched it yet? Start there, then come back. Watch the training

The big ideas from the training

  • Willpower fades. You get fired up at an event, then the feeling wears off and the goal keeps shrinking. Lasting change starts in your subconscious mind.
  • Your life is a tree. The fruits are the life you want: your biggest stresses, solved. The trunk is your power goal. The roots are your subconscious, full of evidence from everything you have been through.
  • Your subconscious speaks emotion, not words. Affirmations, motivational videos and scrolling don't reach it. Feelings do. Money means nothing to it; the feeling money brings is what counts.
  • Pick a goal your mind will accept. Write your 3 to 5 biggest stresses and picture them gone. Those are your fruits. Then choose the goal that would make all of them real. If it feels impossible, your subconscious rejects it and pulls you back like a rubber band.
  • Picture the fruits, not the how. Close your eyes, play emotional music with no words, and for 2 minutes feel your problems as already gone. This drips new evidence into your roots and gives you power and hope.
  • Repeat it, especially when things go wrong. Your mind is a muscle and will slide back at first. When doubt or frustration hits, snap the rubber band, put your headphones in and see your fruits again. Over time you start getting prompts: small ideas of what to do next.
  • Build from vision, not crisis. You don't have to wait for things to fall apart. You can start today.

How this site helps

  • Your own tree. Write your power goal and fruits once. They are saved to your private 3D tree.
  • 2 minutes a day, guided. Press start and close your eyes. Your fruits appear one at a time while the music builds to its peak at the 2-minute mark.
  • Watch your roots change. Each day you do it turns a red root green and grows your tree. Skip days and it starts to dim.
  • As often as you need. Do extra sessions whenever doubt hits. The calendar counts every one.

Before you begin

  1. Find a quiet place where you can be alone and won't be interrupted.
  2. Put on headphones for the deepest experience.
  3. Close your eyes.
  4. Take three slow, deep breaths.
  5. Picture your fruits as if they've already come true. Feel what it's like to have no worries, knowing everything is taken care of.
